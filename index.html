Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my LifeQuest data stored?

LifeQuest currently stores app data locally on your device.

Do I need an account?

No. The current version of LifeQuest does not require an online account.

What happens if I delete the app?

Locally stored progress may be removed when the app is deleted.

How do I report a problem?

Send an email with a description of the issue, your device model, and the version of iOS you are using.

Privacy

LifeQuest does not currently require an account or cloud storage. User progress is stored locally on the device.
