What is LifeQuest?

LifeQuest transforms ordinary productivity into an immersive personal-development adventure. Complete daily and weekly quests, strengthen your habits, improve your fitness, record your journey, and gain experience as you progress.

Whether your goal is to build discipline, improve your health, or become more consistent, LifeQuest helps turn long-term ambitions into achievable daily victories.

Knowledge from the archives

Frequently Asked Questions

Does LifeQuest require an account?

No. The current version of LifeQuest does not require an online account.

Where is my LifeQuest data stored?

The current version stores progress locally on your device.

What happens if I delete the app?

Deleting the app may permanently remove locally stored LifeQuest progress from that device.

How do I report a bug?

Contact support and include a description of the problem, your iPhone model, your iOS version, and the steps that caused the issue.

Is LifeQuest medical or fitness advice?

No. LifeQuest is a productivity and personal-development tool and is not a substitute for professional medical or fitness advice.
