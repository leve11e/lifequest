Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LifeQuest handles information when you use the LifeQuest mobile application.

Information LifeQuest Collects

The current version of LifeQuest does not require users to create an account and does not collect personal information through a LifeQuest cloud account.

Local Device Data

LifeQuest stores app progress and settings locally on your device. This may include:

  • Quest and task information
  • Experience and level progress
  • Fitness assessment results
  • Journal or logbook entries
  • Recovery Mode settings
  • General app preferences

This information remains on your device unless you choose to share it outside the application.

Accounts and Cloud Storage

The current version of LifeQuest does not provide account creation, cloud synchronization, or remote backups.

Data Deletion

Because LifeQuest data is stored locally, deleting the application may permanently remove your locally stored progress and entries.

Notifications

LifeQuest may request permission to send local notifications. You may enable or disable notifications through your device settings.

Third-Party Services

LifeQuest may rely on platform and development services required to distribute and operate the application. The current version does not use advertising networks or sell user information.

Children's Privacy

LifeQuest is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through an online account system.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as LifeQuest gains new features, including possible account, cloud storage, analytics, or online functionality. Any updated policy will be published on this page.
